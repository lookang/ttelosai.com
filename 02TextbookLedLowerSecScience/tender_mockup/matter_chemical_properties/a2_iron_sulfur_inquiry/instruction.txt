Iron and sulfur: mixture or compound?

Safe virtual inquiry based on the SLS video sequence. Learners compare iron and sulfur mixed without heating with the black substance made after a teacher-controlled heating step. They test for an energy change, use a magnet, record what can be physically separated, and use the observations as evidence.

Learning goal: Explain that components in a mixture retain their properties and can be separated physically, whereas a compound is formed by a chemical reaction, has different properties, and is not separated by the physical method used here.
